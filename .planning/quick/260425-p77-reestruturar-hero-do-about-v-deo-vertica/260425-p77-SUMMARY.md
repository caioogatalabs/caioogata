## Decisions Made

- **Layered sticky composition** — Two siblings (video wrapper z-10, headline wrapper z-20) inside the same 400vh container. Both stick at `top: 0` simultaneously. The headline's higher z-index lets the slide-up entry visually pass under it.
- **400vh scroll zone** — Original was 200vh. With 215 frames this gave ~107 px per frame, too fast. 400vh ≈ 215 px per frame at 1080p — feels deliberate.
- **Headline max-width 66%** — cols 9-12 = 33% of grid; reserving 66% for the headline keeps it from running into the video. Validated on the spec ("text wraps at the SAME approximate width because `max-width: 66%` reserves the right third for the video").
- **Hardcoded headline string** — Plan called for "Bridging brand strategy, product craft and technical workflow" (matching home). The previous string was "...and technical implementation". Adding a content key was explicitly out of scope for this quick task.
