I prototype in code, not static files. React, Tailwind, and Vite are my daily stack. My current portfolio is built entirely through LLM-assisted development (Claude Code, Cursor), with scroll-linked animations, WebGL shaders, and a token pipeline, all production code.

Before Azion, I spent 8 years at Huia (digital innovation studio, acquired by Stefanini) leading 40+ projects for Petrobras, Tramontina, and Sicredi. WebGL, interactive campaigns, creative technology under production constraints.
