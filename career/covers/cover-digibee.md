I spent four years designing developer tools at Azion Technologies, an edge computing platform in the same infrastructure layer where Digibee operates. Console, CLI, documentation, public website, design system: I owned every surface technical users touch. Making complex workflows feel simple without hiding the complexity developers need is the problem I know best.

At Azion I built a token-based design system (40+ components, open-source, deployed at Itaú, Magazine Luiza, Netshoes) that cut new screen implementation from days to hours. I care about craft at the level this role demands (typography, spacing, motion) but equally about the system that makes craft reproducible across a platform without degrading over time.

The AI dimension is what makes this role stand out. I don't just design AI experiences, I use AI agents as my primary workflow. Claude Code and Cursor are how I prototype, build, and ship. My portfolio is built entirely through LLM-assisted development. Designing agentic experiences where humans remain in the loop is something I understand from both sides, as a designer defining the patterns and as a daily user navigating the trust boundaries.

B2B products for technical audiences have real constraints. You can't solve a developer's problem with a prettier screen. You solve it with better information architecture, tighter interaction loops, and systems that hold up at scale.
