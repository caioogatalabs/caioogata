## Decisions Made

- **EN shortDescription copy:** Used the example from CONTEXT D-14 verbatim ("Two decades of work for brands across Brazil and beyond.") — concise, scoped to brand + geography breadth, leaves the longer `description` to do the storytelling.
- **PT-BR translation:** Translated to match the existing PT-BR voice in `clients.description` (informal-but-professional, Brazilian Portuguese) — "Duas décadas de trabalho para marcas no Brasil e em diversos países."
- **Type optionality:** Used `shortDescription?: string` (optional) per plan instructions for type tolerance during partial JSON / V1 builds, even though both locale JSONs now contain the field. Wave 2 plans can rely on its presence at runtime.
- **No deletion of removed items' previews:** Per CONTEXT D-03, `previews/{skills,education,clients}.webp` files can stay (no harm). They'll only be loaded if a menu key matches, and those keys no longer exist in `menu.items`.
